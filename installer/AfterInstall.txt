ShapeRack is installed.

Load ShapeRack in FL Studio:

1. Open FL Studio (64-bit).
2. Select Options > Manage plugins.
3. Enable Verify plugins, then click Find installed plugins.
4. Open a Mixer insert's effect slot and select ShapeRack from the plugin list.

The VST3 plugin is installed in the standard Windows VST3 folder:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ShapeRack.vst3

ShapeRack starts with a quarter-note Volume pump. Adjust Amount or Mix to taste.
Select Filter, Pan, or Drive and click Enabled to add another effect. Draw on
each module's graph or choose a preset. Sync follows the host's tempo; Free
sets a rate in Hz.

If you selected CLAP, rescan the CLAP plugin folder in a compatible host:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP

Documentation and licenses are in:
C:\Program Files\PRISMM\ShapeRack

To uninstall, close your audio hosts and remove ShapeRack from Windows
Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Projects using ShapeRack will require
the plugin to remain installed.
